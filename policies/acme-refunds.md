# Acme Retail — Returns, Refunds & Cancellations

## 1. Scope

This policy was last updated on 14 July 2026 and applies to all orders placed on acme.example and in the Acme app where the billing address is in India. Orders placed through a marketplace partner or a third-party storefront are governed by that partner's returns policy and not by this one. Corporate and bulk orders of more than 20 units of a single item are handled under a separate written agreement and fall outside this policy entirely.

## 2. Definitions

"Delivery date" means the date on which our logistics partner records the shipment as delivered, and not the date of dispatch. "Original condition" means that the item is unused, carries all original tags, and is returned in its original packaging, including any protective wrapping.

"Clearance item" means any item listed under Clearance at the time of purchase, or bought during an announced end-of-season sale. "Hygiene seal" means the tamper-evident sticker or wrapper applied to the categories listed in section 5. Where an item carries no hygiene seal, the category exclusions in section 5 do not apply to it.

## 3. Eligibility

A return may be initiated by the account holder who placed the order, or by an authorised user on a shared business account. Proof of purchase is required for every return, in the form of an order ID or the tax invoice emailed at dispatch.

Returns are collected only from the delivery address on the original order. We do not arrange pickup from a different address, and we cannot redirect a collection once it has been scheduled.

## 4. Return window

Returns must be initiated within 30 days of delivery. A return is treated as initiated on the date the request is submitted in the app, and not the date on which the courier collects the item.

Clearance items must be returned within 7 days of delivery. This shorter window applies even where the same item also falls under a category given a longer window elsewhere in this policy, and it is not extended by any promotion.

## 5. Categories excluded from return

The following categories cannot be returned once the hygiene seal has been broken:

- Innerwear and sleepwear, including thermal base layers and shapewear.
- Swimwear and swim accessories, including goggles and swim caps.
- Cosmetics, skincare and fragrance, including unopened gift sets.

An item in an excluded category may still be returned if it is unopened and its hygiene seal is intact. Acme may inspect the seal on collection and reject the return if the seal shows any sign of tampering.

Wearable electronics, including smartwatches and fitness bands, may be returned only if the device has not been registered to an account and all charging accessories are present. Wearable electronics are not a hygiene-sealed category and are not covered by the exclusions listed above.

## 6. Condition of returned items

A return is accepted only if the item is in original condition, the return was initiated within the applicable window, and the item is not listed in section 5 as excluded. All three conditions must be met, and satisfying two of the three is not sufficient.

Opened electronics that are otherwise eligible are subject to a restocking fee of 15% of the item price, deducted from the refund. Items returned with visible damage that was not reported within 48 hours of delivery are rejected at inspection and sent back to the customer at their own cost.

## 7. Refunds

Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 5 business days of the returned item passing inspection. Orders paid by cash on delivery are refunded as Acme Wallet credit rather than to a bank account, and wallet credit cannot be withdrawn as cash.

Shipping charges are not refundable, except where the return is due to a manufacturing defect or to an incorrect item having been shipped. In those two cases the original shipping charge is refunded in full.

## 8. Cancellations

An order may be cancelled at no charge at any time before it is dispatched. Once an order has been dispatched it cannot be cancelled, and must instead be processed as a return under section 4.

## 9. Exchanges

An exchange for a different size of the same item may be requested once per order, within the return window in section 4, and is subject to stock availability. Exchanges are not available for clearance items.

## 10. Escalation

If a return request is declined, the decision may be escalated to support@acme.example within 14 days of the decision. An escalation does not extend the return window in section 4, and a declined escalation is final.
